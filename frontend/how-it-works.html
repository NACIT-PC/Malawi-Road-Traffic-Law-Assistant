The answer pipeline

How it works

The assistant uses a backend retrieval-and-generation pipeline. Its response is informational and depends on the legal records made available to the service.

  1. 1

    Ask a general question

    Submit a road traffic law question without personal or payment details.

  2. 2

    Retrieve available evidence

    The backend searches its configured legal corpus. Only records marked verified and current are eligible for answer generation.

  3. 3

    Review the response and citations

    When evidence is available, the response includes citations that can be inspected through the Legal Sources page. When evidence is insufficient, the backend returns a limitation response instead of a supported answer.

Current prototype limitations

The API currently returns supported or insufficient-evidence results. Automated conflict detection, outdated-source detection, clarification questions, structured penalty lookup, and official next-step lookup are not implemented. A response must not be interpreted as confirmation of an applicable fine.
