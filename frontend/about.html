About this service

About & limitations

This prototype helps people explore information about Malawi road traffic law using records supplied to its backend.

How sources are used

The backend retrieves relevant entries from its configured legal corpus and uses eligible evidence to prepare a response. Citations are provided so you can inspect the identified source and provision. A citation does not by itself establish that a rule applies to your circumstances.

Some questions cannot be answered reliably if the corpus has no relevant verified record. In that case, the assistant should state that evidence is insufficient rather than invent a rule or penalty.

Important limitations

  • This is general legal information, not legal advice or an official determination.
  • The assistant does not determine guilt, issue or cancel fines, process payments, or confirm that a fine has been paid.
  • The current API does not implement conflict detection, outdated-source detection, clarification prompts, structured penalty lookup, or official next-step lookup.
  • Do not rely on an answer as an official notice. Confirm important details through an authorized official channel.
  • Do not enter personal identification, driving licence, or payment information.

Report a problem

Answer screens provide a reporting form for incorrect answers or citations. The current backend validates reports but does not store them and responds with an unavailable error. A report is not submitted unless the interface explicitly confirms success; do not include personal or sensitive information.
