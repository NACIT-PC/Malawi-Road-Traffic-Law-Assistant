# Data Processing Agreement

**Status:** Conditional business-to-business template; incomplete and unsigned  
**Prepared:** 14 November 2026  
**Contact:** [insert monitored legal/privacy contact]

> This template applies only where MRTLA processes personal data for an organizational customer under that customer's documented instructions. It is not an agreement for ordinary public users.

## 1. Parties and applicability

Complete and sign this draft with the customer's full legal name and address as Controller and the legal entity operating MRTLA as Processor. Insert registration details, signatories, and an effective date. If MRTLA determines its own purposes and means for a processing activity, identify that activity and its correct legal roles instead of assuming this Processor model applies.

This DPA is not effective until completed, reviewed, and signed by both parties, and only applies to the processing described in its completed Schedule 1.

## 2. Instructions and purpose

The Processor will process personal data only on the Controller's documented instructions and for the specific purpose and duration recorded in Schedule 1, unless applicable law requires otherwise. The customer must not submit data beyond the agreed scope.

## 3. Confidentiality and security

Document appropriate technical and organizational measures in Schedule 2 before processing begins. Do not treat the current prototype's controls as a complete security commitment. Authorized personnel must be bound by role-appropriate confidentiality obligations.

## 4. Sub-processors and transfers

List every approved sub-processor, service, processing location, purpose, and transfer mechanism in Schedule 3. Google Gemini may receive submitted question text when answer generation is needed; hosting and infrastructure providers depend on deployment. Confirm provider terms, authorization, safeguards, and applicable cross-border transfer requirements before enabling processing.

## 5. Assistance, incidents, and audits

Agree how the Processor will assist with data-subject requests, security incidents, impact assessments, regulator inquiries, and audits, including response times and reasonable evidence requirements. Complete incident contacts, notice periods, audit limits, and procedures in the schedules; this template makes no incident-response guarantee.

## 6. Return, deletion, and end of service

At the end of the services, the Controller must choose in Schedule 1 whether data is returned or deleted, subject to legal retention duties and technically documented backups. Define deletion confirmation and backup expiry before signing.

## 7. Required schedules

**Schedule 1 — Processing details and instructions:** subject matter, duration, nature and purpose, data subjects, personal-data categories, sensitive-data status, Controller instructions, and return/deletion choice.

**Schedule 2 — Security measures:** verified technical and organizational measures, access controls, encryption, backup, incident handling, and relevant responsibilities.

**Schedule 3 — Sub-processors and transfers:** approved provider legal names, services, locations, purposes, transfer safeguards, and authorization process.

Add liability, governing law, dispute, cooperation, and signature provisions only after legal review. This is an incomplete drafting template, not a signed agreement or a compliance certification. A qualified privacy lawyer must adapt it to the parties, applicable law, deployed vendors, and actual data flows.
