## Cookies used by this app

Based on the current application code, MRTLA does not intentionally set first-party cookies for analytics, advertising, or login. Verify this against the production site and all hosting, security, and embedded third-party services before relying on this statement.

## Local storage preference

The app saves your light/dark display preference in browser local storage using `mrtla-theme`. It is stored on your device and used to restore your selected appearance. Clear it through your browser's site-data settings.

## Third parties and browser controls

The backend may call a configured AI provider, and deployed hosting or security layers may use their own storage technologies. Their behavior depends on the final configuration and must be checked before launch. Browser settings can block or clear cookies and local storage, though this may affect functionality.

## Changes and consent

If cookies or similar technologies are added, update this policy and obtain any consent required by applicable law before non-essential storage is used. This draft requires legal and production-configuration review.
